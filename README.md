# 🚦 RouteX

## AI-Powered Multi-Camera Vehicle Intelligence & Urban Traffic Analytics

RouteX is an intelligent computer-vision platform that transforms data from multiple CCTV cameras into **connected vehicle intelligence and actionable urban traffic insights**.

The system combines **vehicle detection, ANPR/OCR, multi-object tracking, vehicle re-identification, cross-camera matching, trajectory reconstruction, traffic analytics, and alert detection** into a unified platform.

Instead of analyzing CCTV cameras independently, RouteX connects vehicle observations across different camera locations to reconstruct how vehicles move throughout a city.
